## 📖 Story Mode Progression Guide

Story Mode is divided into two major acts: **Bit Synthesis** and **Resolution Scaling**.

### Act 1: The Oscilloscope (Bits 1-10)
Your goal is to reach full 10-bit dimensionality on a 1D oscilloscope.
- **Phase 1 (The Void)**: You start with 0 bits. Collect **1 Hz** and **1 V** to buy the initial **N=1** point.
- **Phase 2 (Synthesis)**: Collect resources to auto-compile Bit-Depth upgrades.
- **Phase 3 (The Sovereign)**: Upon reaching Bit 9, you are besieged by the **Corrupted Sovereign (Boss 1)**.
- **Phase 4 (Dimensionality)**: Defeat the boss to compile Bit 10 and unlock the **Master Engine**.

### Act 2: The Resolution (Res 1-10)
After mastering dimensionality, you must scale the engine's temporal resolution (Frame Rate).
- **Phase 1 (Optimization)**: Collect Baud (Wavelength), Hz, and V to auto-compile Resolution upgrades.
- **Phase 2 (The Fracture)**: Reach Resolution 9 (144 FPS) to encounter the **Fracture Sovereign (Boss 2)**.
- **Victory (Transcendence)**: Defeat the final boss to unlock Resolution 10 (240 FPS) and complete the Story.

## 🕹️ Mode Mechanics

### Arcade Mode Difficulty Scaling
- **Bits 1-5**: Slow Waltz (Easy Zone) - Low speed, simple patterns.
- **Bits 6-8**: Arcade Techno (Regular Zone) - Faster pulses, variable rhythms.
- **Bit 9+**: Digital Hardcore (Crazy Zone) - Aggressive spikes and error zones.

### Survival Mode
- **Status**: God Mode Lite.
- **Starting Gear**: All Bits (10) and Resolutions (10) unlocked instantly.
- **Overshields**: Starts with 5.
- **Progression**: Endless. Difficulty scales past "Crazy" into uncharted error spikes.

### Polarizer Guide
- **UP (Crest)**: Use to absorb high-amplitude pulses.
- **DOWN (Trough)**: Use to absorb low-amplitude pulses.
- **NEUTRAL (Bit 8+)**: Special mode to absorb packets and restore HP before bosses.
